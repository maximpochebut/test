Biography

A Young Voice of Extraordinary Talent

Margarita Pochebut is an exceptional young Ukrainian violinist. At just six years old, Margarita attracted international attention when she won the Yelman Grand Prix at the International Violin Competition in 2016.

In 2018, she was successful with 1st prizes at the 6th International Violin Competition in Prague, the 22nd International Competition Bohdan Warchal "Talents for Europe", and in 2019 at the 25th International Young Virtuoso Competition in Zagreb. She won 2nd prize at the 10th Louis Spohr International Competition for Young Violinists in 2022, and twice became the laureate at Il Piccolo Violino Magico competition (2019 and 2024) in Italy.

Since the age of six, Margarita has been touring with prestigious orchestras and performing in renowned concert halls in Ukraine, Italy, Belgium, France, Switzerland, Austria, Estonia, the Czech Republic and Slovakia — alongside her ongoing musical education.

Margarita plays an exceptional violin by Lorenzo Storioni, Cremona 1784, provided by the Stretton Society. She refined her talent at the prestigious International Menuhin Music Academy in Rolle (2022–2024) and now continues her studies at the Mozarteum University in Salzburg as the youngest bachelor student in the class of Benjamin Schmid.

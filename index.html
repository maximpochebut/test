Biography

A Voice Through the Strings

Sofia Marchetti discovered her passion for the violin at the age of five in Milan, Italy. Under the guidance of maestro Luca Ferretti at the Giuseppe Verdi Conservatory, she developed a technique rooted in the Italian tradition — expressive, nuanced, and deeply human.

After graduating with honours, Sofia pursued advanced studies at the Royal Conservatoire of Brussels and later at the Kronberg Academy in Germany, where she refined her interpretative approach working alongside some of the world's foremost string players.

Today she performs as a soloist and chamber musician across Europe, regularly appearing with orchestras and at prestigious festivals. Her repertoire spans baroque to contemporary works, and she is equally at home on the concert stage as in intimate salon settings.

Beyond performing, Sofia is passionate about education and mentorship — she gives masterclasses at conservatories and summer academies, and is an advocate for bringing classical music to new and diverse audiences.
